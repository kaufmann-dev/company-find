`FCFF = EBIT × (1 − operating tax rate) + D&A − all-in capital expenditure − change in operating working capital`. Discount each remaining annual cash flow to 8 September 2026 at its scenario nominal USD WACC. Equity equals operating EV plus excess corporate cash/nonoperating assets less debt and other claims; common value cannot be negative. Present fair value divides by current diluted shares.

For conventional DCFs,2036–40 scales sales/costs/D&A with terminal growth and holds margins fixed. Reinvestment gradually approaches the greater of 2035 net reinvestment and `NOPAT × g / terminal ROIC`. The larger existing replacement requirement is retained as a floor; it does not disappear because a terminal formula assumes accounting D&A equals maintenance investment. Terminal FCFF uses this mature investment burden. This explicit analytical transition avoids an unexplained cash-flow step; it remains a valuation assumption. GSL uses an explicit finite fleet residual instead. AerCap uses FCFE after required equity investment, described separately.

Discount rates are judgmental required returns, not inferred market forecasts or fitted historical betas. The latest available official 10-year Treasury observation was 4.78% on 4 September 2026; 5-year was 4.54%. Operating and financing risks justify the additional spread, with sensitivities shown. Terminal nominal growth generally 1–3% is below early demand growth; ROIC assumptions require reinvestment. Declared distributions to prior holders are excluded. A positive IRR can coexist with a zero present common residual if dividends precede loss of exit equity.
