**Investment research — 8 September 2026**

**No confirmed buy recommendations emerge from this research.** Comcast is the strongest unconfirmed valuation candidate; PayPal is near fair value. The other seven detailed valuations offer insufficient prospective cash flow at the captured prices. This is a finding about the investigated universe, not proof that global equities contain no bargains.

The screen investigated **44 companies across ten economic chains**, including foreign listings and smaller specialists, and built **nine detailed valuations before selection**. The most consequential forecast differences are: electrical-equipment supply responds to high returns; replacement and financing consume much of apparently cheap transport earnings; payment volumes can grow faster than retained fees; and cable cash generation can outlast subscriber losses, but only if bundle monetization and investment economics hold.

**What is worth further investigation**

Comcast's approximately $31 weighted value against $26.12 suggests 19.6% upside, but only 16.4% margin of safety. The base value is about $33; the joint adverse scenario exhausts present common residual value, although dividends received before the five-year exit prevent a 100% loss of invested cash in that return scenario. Debt and other claims amplify relatively modest operating differences. The base requires broadband revenue per subscriber to recover after 2027, substantial wireless growth, and gradual improvement in a post-Versant media business. These assumptions are not demonstrated by one successful sports quarter. The evidence supports further research, not a confirmed recommendation. [Comcast June 10-Q, filed 23 July 2026, segments and transactions](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1166691/000162828026049360/cmcsa-20260630.htm), [23 July pro forma trends, pp 1–3](https://www.cmcsa.com/static-files/b269b35e-94ff-43d2-8f05-858c92bb7488).

The forecast values all economic claims a current Comcast shareholder would retain through the announced NBCUniversal/Sky separation, with no speculative rerating premium. It excludes already separated Versant. The up-to£1.6 bn ITV commitment is charged to 2027 investment at the captured GBP/USD rate; potential acquired earnings are a separate sensitivity. Deferred compensation, minority interests, capitalized compensation and investment commitments are included. Temporary cash-tax benefits do not become perpetual cash flow. [Comcast 2025 annual report,3 February 2026, benefits and investment notes](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1166691/000162828026004994/cmcsa-20251231.htm), [29 June separation presentation](https://www.cmcsa.com/static-files/ced447a4-4823-4904-b398-bab2a392d1c7).

“The current price can be justified if” base revenues and capital needs deliver about 16.7% group EBIT margin in 2030. “My forecast differs because” wireless/business connectivity and parks partly offset video and broadband erosion, producing 18.7%. “The evidence supporting that difference is” installed customer relationships and current wireless adoption, tempered by weak post-separation media profitability and funded competing fiber capacity. “The resulting difference in value is” about $7 per share against the base case, but only $5.13 probability weighted. This is one reverse-DCF combination, not a uniquely identified market forecast. Directly available, consistently dated consensus was not obtained. [AT&T22 July 2026 results, fiber targets and capital plan](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/732717/000073271726000294/t-2q2026exhibit991.htm).

Attempting to disprove Comcast's apparent discount is decisive: holding broadband monthly revenue per subscriber at $70, adding $1 bn recurring separation costs and increasing all capital spending 15% lowers base value to about $15.12. Merely transferring ten probability points from upside to downside lowers weighted value to $23.90. Approximately seven points would eliminate the observed price gap. Until renewal cohorts show economically profitable bundle retention and standalone costs are clearer, that uncertainty can reverse the conclusion. These are calculated stresses, not management or consensus forecasts. The individual and combined tests are in [additional_checks.json](research/connectivity/additional_checks.json).

PayPal's weighted value of about $54 against $53.25 provides little protection despite a base case near $60. The reverse valuation needs approximately 14.9% 2030 EBIT margin, versus 16.5% in the base forecast: the market price already tolerates substantial operational deterioration. Better execution could generate good returns, but a modest change in probabilities or required return removes the gap. TPV growth alone does not validate the thesis; retained transaction-margin dollars and capital needs matter. [PayPal 28 July 2026 results, transaction-margin and cash-flow tables](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1633917/000163391726000080/pypl2q-26earningsrelease.htm).

Mueller and AerCap have stronger resilience or more directly contracted economics than several high-upside narratives, but neither is demonstrably discounted. Powell and IQVIA are attractive capability owners whose prices require substantially better sustained economics than the base forecasts. CF's gas advantage does not eliminate global supply or project-investment risk. Global Ship Lease's contracted cash must fund replacement ships. Fiserv's apparent earnings cheapness weakens materially after financed capital additions, compensation and debt are included. These are investment rejections at these prices, not short-sale recommendations.

**Research basis and limits**

The five-year holding period is 8 September 2026–8 September 2031. Company-specific annual operating forecasts cover 2026–35; conventional DCFs then explicitly transition through 2040. Global Ship Lease instead uses a finite 2035 fleet residual; AerCap uses a capital-consistent equity DCF through 2035. The remaining 2026 fiscal year is prorated by days, a disclosed approximation that does not resolve seasonality.

The final Massive snapshot was captured at 15:18:28 UTC on 8 September 2026. All nine modeled quotes use the 15:02 UTC minute close except GSL, whose last observed minute was 15:00 UTC. These are delayed intraday observations, not executable real-time quotes or closing prices. Exact raw timestamps and fields are in [quote_capture.json](outputs/quote_capture.json). USD is both cash-flow and discount-rate currency for the nine valuations. The ITV translation uses Massive GBP/USD1.3546 from the 7 September daily bar; future transaction FX is uncertain. Ordinary foreign listings remain investigated comparisons where a native-share/FX/corporate-action bridge was not verified.

Latest annual/interim disclosures supply share counts, debt, cash and claims. June balances and July/August cover share counts are aligned for disclosed events; undisclosed subsequent buybacks and treasury movements remain unknown, not imputed facts. Powell's 3-for-1 April split is reflected in both price and shares. Basic market capitalization and diluted intrinsic value are separately reported; customer and restricted cash are not freely added to equity. Company-specific reconciliation notes accompany every model.

Available tools were Massive market snapshots, corporate-action/reference and FX endpoints; web access to primary filings, issuer/customer/competitor disclosures and official statistics; Python calculations; and independent parallel research/model review. Optional standardized-statement endpoints returned entitlement errors; they were not retried or used. The planned primary-source financial basis remained accessible. No broker-consensus dataset, complete current plant-capacity database or verified global CRO-capacity series was available.

The screen breadth and nine valuations are complete, but **full verification of bottleneck timing and the leading positive valuation candidate remains incomplete**. Equipment, CRO and small-container supply balances include visibly labeled assumptions; they are scenario stress tests, not measured capacity censuses. Non-US valuation coverage is shallower than US-listed coverage. These limits prevent a confirmed positive recommendation and any claim to have found the objectively strongest investments worldwide.

**Shared dependencies and observable tests**

Infrastructure budgets and construction labor link Powell and Mueller. Fuel, financing and usable capacity link CF and transport economics. Household spending and bargaining link payments and Comcast. Higher discount rates harm all long-duration valuations; leverage magnifies operating disappointments at Fiserv, Comcast and the lessors. Owning several such names would not eliminate shared refinancing or customer-budget risk.

By 2027, Comcast needs evidence of profitable bundle retention and credible separation costs; sustained broadband revenue per subscriber near $70 plus increased investment invalidates the base valuation. PayPal needs growth in transaction-margin dollars excluding balance interest; two weak quarters despite TPV growth challenge the recovery. AerCap requires near 97% or better utilization and roughly 11% portfolio rent yield; two quarters below 95% weaken the thesis. Mueller needs improving shipment volumes and cash conversion. Powell needs repeated profitable orders after competing capacity ramps, not just backlog conversion. CF needs budget-consistent Blue Point execution and a durable gas spread; GSL needs funded newbuild deliveries and acceptable refinancing before old charters roll. IQVIA needs bookings converted into profitable, funded work; Fiserv needs retention and all-in cash investment to improve. Each detailed case supplies adverse values and quantitative invalidation conditions.

Unconfirmed alternatives deserving a separate completed valuation are ICON (control remediation and cash conversion), Mosaic/ICL (sulfur costs, fertilizer affordability and asset claims), and selected foreign maintenance/water specialists. No valuation is assigned where evidence is missing. The screen records the strongest counterarguments, including why market leaders and exciting growth stories were rejected or left unresolved.
